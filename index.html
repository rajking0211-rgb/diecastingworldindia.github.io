How Your Diecast Models Are Packed

We know the heartbreak of cracked blister bubbles, bent card hangers, and cracked acrylic cases. Here is our 5-layer guarantee:

1

Card Protectors

Clamshell protector casing fitted on cards to shield all 4 edges.

2

High-Density Bubble

Minimum 5 wraps of heavy shock-absorbing air cushioning.

3

Edge Corner Guards

Stiffened cardboard angle guards guarding carton corners.

4

5-Ply Hard Box

Double-wall corrugated shipping carton resistant to drop pressure.

5

Fragile Security Tape

Bright red 'Fragile Scale Model - Handle with Care' tamper seal.
