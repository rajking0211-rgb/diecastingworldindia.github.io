## 🌟 Key Collector Features

1. **"The Collector Garage" Hold Bag (Castly & Torque Tribe Inspired)**:
   - Collectors can lock models across weekly drops and hold them safely for up to 45 days at the Mumbai hub.
   - Combine multiple orders into one shipping box to save courier fees.
2. **Direct WhatsApp 1-Click Ordering**:
   - Send carts or single items directly to Deepa Shah via WhatsApp with auto-formatted model names, scales, SKUs, and delivery address.
3. **5-Layer Mint Packaging Armor**:
   - Card protector clamshell, high-density bubble wrap, cardboard corner edge guards, 5-ply outer box, and fragile caution tape.
4. **Pre-Order Drops Calendar with Token Booking**:
   - Reserve international drops (Mini GT, Kaido House, Pop Race, Inno64) with a small token (₹299–₹499); balance due upon Mumbai arrival.
5. **Anti-Scalper Fair Play Safeguards**:
   - 1-2 unit limits on hyped chase castings and RLC models.
6. **Live Instant Search & Multi-Filters**:
   - Scale (1:64, 1:43, 1:18, Dioramas), Brand, Stock Status, and Instant Keyword Search (Shortcut `/`).
7. **Instagram Live Showcase**:
   - Integrated visual feed from `@diecast_world_india`.
